# Short script — five levels

## LEVEL 1 — MINING ZONE

**Pre-level text:**

The first mining fields are operating as planned. Before we go any further, we need
the area cleared of the larger clusters of rock and debris.

Control confirms: no threat.

This is a routine mission.

**Gameplay:** asteroids only. Large rocks break into smaller fragments. Once the
field is clear, the level is complete.

---

## LEVEL 2 — SENSOR ANOMALY

**Pre-level text:**

The operators have noticed strange interference. Several times now our sensors have
picked up large objects that were not in the previous scan.

They appear for a fraction of a second and are gone.

Control maintains it is a navigation system fault.

**Gameplay:** still asteroids, but from time to time an alien ship appears on
screen. It is visible only very briefly, then vanishes. It does not attack. It may
even appear in a place where a moment earlier there was nothing.

The objective is still to clear the field.

---

## LEVEL 3 — CONTACT

**Pre-level text:**

This is not a system fault.

The last scan showed several unidentified craft. We cannot determine their origin
or their construction.

Worse, not all of them appeared on radar.

If you meet them — be careful.

We do not know what they want.

**Gameplay:** the first real encounters with the aliens. At first there are only a
few, and they appear without warning. When the player's ship comes too close, the
aliens engage.

---

## LEVEL 4 — HUNT

**Pre-level text:**

We have lost contact with the remaining craft.

This is no longer accidental contact.

Alien ships are patrolling the entire area and reacting to the presence of our
craft.

Do not try to fight them all at once.

Find a way through the rings.

**Gameplay:** many alien ships spread across a large field. Once they detect the
player they give chase. The player can fight, avoid contact, or simply make it to
the end of the level.

---

## LEVEL 5 — ESCAPE

**Pre-level text:**

Saturnian control zone extends far beyond our original maps.

We no longer know how many alien craft are in this area.

There is no time for further investigation.

Stay alive and get outside the anomaly zone.

It is the last known way home.

**Gameplay:** a huge number of alien ships, relentless pursuit and combat. The
player has to cross an enormous area, or destroy every hostile craft.

---

## The shared premise of the game world

Every level takes place in a large, looping area — far bigger than the screen. A
pilot leaving the area on one side reappears on the opposite side.

At first this looks like a plain arcade convention. Over time, though, the game can
suggest that the SATURNium anomaly causes a **local curvature of space**, so that
distant parts of the area become connected to one another.

The player never has to be given the full explanation.

What matters is that the space around Saturn starts behaving **less and less
normally**.
