# Intro

THE YEAR IS 2093.

Unmanned probes sent by the largest corporations on Earth discover an extraordinary
mineral in the rings of Saturn. It is given the working name **SATURNium**. The
material has properties never observed before — among them an effect on the
propagation of electromagnetic and gravitational waves, which raises hopes of
building the drive of the future.

Before long, small problems begin to appear near the new mining fields. Automated
ships start reporting false readings. Objects show up on radar out of nowhere. Some
craft collide with something that was not there a moment earlier.

Eight crewed ships are assigned to investigate the anomaly.

You fly one of them.

Your task is to clear one region of the rings after another and press on.

Except that something here is decidedly **not the way it should be**.
